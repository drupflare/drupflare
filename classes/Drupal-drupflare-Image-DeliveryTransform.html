\Drupal\drupflare\Image DeliveryTransform

An image style's effect chain as the one delivery-time transform the Worker renders.

The toolkit writes no derivative, so a style reaches a visitor only through the Worker's delivery path. A chain this cannot express as one transform answers null and keeps its stock URL.
