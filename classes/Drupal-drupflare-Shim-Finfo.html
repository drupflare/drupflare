Shim

\Drupal\drupflare\Shim Finfo

The finfo class in PHP, by magic bytes, for a build without ext-fileinfo.

Upload validators and flysystem's MIME detector ask one question: what type is this content. libmagic answers it from a database of thousands of signatures; this class carries the ones a CMS upload meets (images, PDF, office formats, archives, audio, video, text) and answers application/octet-stream for anything else, which is libmagic's own answer for unknown bytes. Office Open XML is told apart from a plain zip by its [Content_Types].xml member and top-level directory, the way libmagic does it.
