\Drupal\drupflare\Cache CfwCacheBackendFactory

Hands out {@link CfwCacheBackend} instead of core's database backend.

Core's factory constructs DatabaseBackend directly rather than resolving a class, so a subclass of the FACTORY is the seam. Everything else -- the connection, the checksum provider, the serializer, the clock and the per-bin row cap -- is read from the parent, so a site that has configured database_cache_max_rows keeps its setting.
