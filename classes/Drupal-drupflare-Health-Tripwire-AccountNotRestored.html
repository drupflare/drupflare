Tripwire

\Drupal\drupflare\Health\Tripwire AccountNotRestored

Asserts the account switcher stack is empty when the request ends.

The other half of the uid-1 disclosure. A switch that is not popped leaves the NEXT request running as whoever the last one switched to, and on a persistent interpreter that is not a theoretical hazard -- it is the mechanism that put admin HTML into the anonymous page cache. A non-empty stack is therefore critical rather than a warning.
