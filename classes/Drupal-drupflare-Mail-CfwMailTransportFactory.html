\Drupal\drupflare\Mail CfwMailTransportFactory

Builds {@see CfwMailTransport} for a `cfwmail://` DSN.

Tagged mailer.transport_factory, which symfony_mailer collects into its transport factory manager. Without that module the tag is inert.
