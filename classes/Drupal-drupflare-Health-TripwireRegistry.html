Health

\Drupal\drupflare\Health TripwireRegistry

The PHP-side tripwires, and the one place they are enumerated.

The host half in src/ops/supervisor.ts holds the checks PHP structurally cannot make: a JS throw out of a wasm import, its own isolate being killed, or wasm linear memory rising. This half holds the ones that need Drupal's own state, which the host cannot see.
