Exec

\Drupal\drupflare\Exec Net

Serves wget and curl for one URL, over the fetch transport.

Anything that would need more than one request or a feature the transport lacks is refused by its option name: recursion and mirroring for wget, and multiple URLs, globbing, uploads, proxies and disabled certificate checks for curl.
